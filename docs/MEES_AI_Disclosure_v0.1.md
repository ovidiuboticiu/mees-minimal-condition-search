# Generative-AI Disclosure

OpenAI ChatGPT (OpenAI; **GPT-5.6 Sol**; accessed August 9, 2026; https://chatgpt.com/) was used extensively in this research project for interactive ideation, experiment-design assistance, code generation and revision, statistical-analysis assistance, literature-search support, first-pass figure generation, manuscript drafting and editing, and construction of the reproducibility package.

The human author retained responsibility for research decisions, approval of frozen protocols and success criteria, interpretation of all results, preservation of negative findings, claim selection, citation checking, and the final manuscript. Generative AI is not listed as an author.

The reported “independent concordance” for EXT-3 refers to a second computational implementation of the ground-truth calculation within the same project; it does not constitute independent replication by an external research team.

A formal citation to the AI system is included in the manuscript bibliography.
